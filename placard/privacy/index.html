Privacy Policy for Placard

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Placard is an Android app that displays text at a large size on your screen. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version is that it does not collect any.

Data we collect

None. Placard does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data about you.

Permissions

Placard requests no permissions that give it access to anything of yours: not your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or network. You will never be asked to grant Placard a permission.

If you inspect the app's manifest, you may see one entry ending in DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. It is added automatically by Google's Android support library, grants access to nothing, and exists only to keep the app's internal messages private on older versions of Android.

Network access

Placard itself does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. The app works identically in aeroplane mode. Because Placard never sends anything anywhere, there is no server that could hold information about you.

Ratings through Google Play

Placard includes one Google library, Google Play In-App Review. After you have used full screen a few times, Placard asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether to show its standard rating window; Google Play decides whether it appears. If you leave a rating or review, it goes directly to Google Play under Google's own terms. Placard never learns whether you rated it or what you wrote.

Information kept on your device

So that the app opens the way you left it, Placard saves the following to your device's private app storage:

  • the last message you typed;
  • your colour, typeface, scrolling and flashing choices;
  • how many times you have used full screen, which decides when the gesture hints appear and when to ask for a rating.

This data never leaves your device. It is not included in Android's automatic cloud backup. No other app can read it. It is deleted permanently when you uninstall Placard, and you can clear it at any time through Settings › Apps › Placard › Storage › Clear data.

Children

Placard is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Placard handles information differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
