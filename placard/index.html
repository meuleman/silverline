Placard

A sign that says “I’m here”.

Type a message. Tap once. It fills the whole screen, as large as it will go, in any colours you choose. Then hold it up and let the person you are waiting for find you.

What it’s for

  • Airport pickups — a passenger’s name instead of a folded sheet of paper.
  • Crowds — festivals, stadiums, anywhere a phone call won’t work.
  • Marches — a sign you can rewrite in seconds that still reads after dark.
  • Being understood — when speaking isn’t an option.

What it doesn’t do

  • No adverts, now or later.
  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No Android permissions at all.
  • No internet connection used or needed.
  • Every colour free. No upgrade, no subscription.
